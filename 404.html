About ACNEI

The Africa Computational Neuroscience Educational Initiative (ACNEI) is dedicated to expanding computational neuroscience education and early exposure across Africa through innovative training, mentorship, and collaboration.

Our mission is to build a vibrant community of computational neuroscience researchers and educators across the African continent, fostering cutting-edge research and education in brain science.
