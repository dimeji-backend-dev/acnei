Our Aim, Objectives & Vision

We’re expanding computational neuroscience knowledge and early exposure across Africa—so more young scholars can learn, grow, and contribute.

Our Aim

ACNEI aims to expand computational neuroscience knowledge, education, and early exposure across Africa. By collaborating with established institutions and programs, we broaden access, strengthen representation, and support young African scholars to contribute to local and global scientific progress.

Our Objectives

  • Build a strong foundation in computational science for promising young scholars across Africa.
  • Provide accessible, high-quality training and mentorship through virtual and hybrid programs.
  • Increase representation, participation, and expertise in computational neuroscience across the continent.
  • Promote diversity, equity, and inclusion in neuroscience research, training, and academic communities across Africa.

Our Vision

A future where young Africans actively contribute to global computational neuroscience—driving discovery, innovation, and projects that create meaningful impact.

Our Programs

Four practical pathways for learning, research, and community building in computational neuroscience across Africa.

NeuroComp Africa

Early exposure and hands-on learning for high school and undergraduate learners—through regional hubs, mentorship, and guided projects.

Pan-African Research Network

Connecting researchers, institutions, and resources across Africa to support collaboration, shared learning, and joint research.

Open-Source Neuroscience Tools

Free tools and beginner-friendly resources—scripts, notebooks, tutorials, and low-cost hardware ideas—to support learning and research.

Workshop Series

Virtual sessions for students, educators, and early-career researchers—practical, beginner-friendly, and focused on real research skills.

Why Invest in African Computational Neuroscience?

Your support enables groundbreaking research with global implications while building sustainable scientific capacity across Africa.

  • Unique Research Opportunities: Africa's genetic diversity offers unparalleled insights
  • High Impact: 15:1 return on investment through publications and innovations
  • Sustainable Model: Capacity building and local leadership for long-term impact
  • Interdisciplinary Approach: Bridging neuroscience, AI, and data science
  • Proven Track Record: Successfully implemented across 12 African countries
